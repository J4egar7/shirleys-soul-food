How pickup works

  1. Call in your plate by 11:00 if you want it waiting at 11:30.
  2. Come to the counter and give your name.
  3. Pay at the register, grab your tray, and go.

Office lunches, church groups, and family gatherings are our specialty. Call at least a day ahead so we can fry enough chicken and bake enough cornbread.
