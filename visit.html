We serve one sitting only and close when the line runs out. Holiday weeks vary — call first.

Getting here

  1. From downtown Toccoa: head west on Currahee Street from the courthouse; we're a few blocks down on the left.
  2. From US-123 / US-17: take the Toccoa exit into town and follow signs for Currahee Street.
  3. Parking: street parking on Currahee, plus a lot behind the building. Midday fills up — 11:30 is the sweet spot.

Good to know

Cash and card accepted at the counter. Tray service, no table reservations — walk in and get in line. Big groups should call ahead so we can have enough chicken down.

Call (706) 297-7739 for large orders or to ask what's on the line.

Seats go quick
First time?

Walk in, take a tray, point at what looks good.

There's no host stand and no menu to study at the table. The line is the menu. Pick a meat, then three or four vegetables, and somebody will carry your tray if you need a hand.
